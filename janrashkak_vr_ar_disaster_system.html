Real-World Challenges

Identified Critical Disaster Problems

Traditional disaster management fails due to late information, lack of practical training, and panic. Here is how JANRASHKAK categorizes and addresses real-world bottlenecks.

1. Navigation & Evacuation

  • Rescue Delays: Submerged roads block traditional emergency responders.
  • Transportation Breakdown: Commute routes get flooded without notice.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: AR Safe Route navigation overlay showing nearest relief shelters even offline.

2. Power & Electrical Safety

  • Electric Short-Circuits: Submerged transformers cause electrocution risks.
  • Electricity Outages & Lighting: Total dark out in street lighting during storm nights.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: Main switch cutoff alerts & danger zone mapping.

3. Health, Disease & Panic

  • Panic & Confusion: Lack of practical pre-event drills causes chaos.
  • Disease & Water Quality: Outbreak of cholera, dengue & contaminated drinking water.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: VR practical drill immersive muscle memory training.

4. Livestock & Weather Impact

  • Livestock Safety: Farmers lose cattle due to lack of elevated animal pen alerts.
  • Weather & Crop Information: Unexpected inundation ruins harvests.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: Rural localized SMS warnings + fodder & cattle safe zone maps.

5. Food & Sanitation

  • Food Shortages: Ration distribution centers get cut off.
  • Waste Management: Floating waste clogs drainage channels.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: Ration relief hub tracking & waste hazard reporting points.

6. Digital Literacy & Skill

  • Digital Literacy Gap: Complex apps are hard to use for rural populations.
  • Employment & Recovery: Loss of livelihood post disaster.
  • JANRASHKAK Fix: Simple icon-driven UI, multi-lingual audio guidance & school VR programs.
Core Technologies

VR Training & AR Navigation Modules

JANRASHKAK operates in two primary modes to ensure maximum survival probability before and during a flood event.

Module 01

VR Training Module (Pre-Disaster)

Using affordable VR headsets (e.g., Google Cardboard, VR Box), schools and community centers can conduct realistic simulations without physical danger.

Pre-Flood Preparation: Practice assembling 72-hour survival kits in VR.
Evacuation Drills: Learn how to safely navigate rushing water without panicking.
Helping Vulnerable Groups: Rescue protocols for children, elderly, and livestock.
Electrical Safety: Simulated identification of hazardous live wires.
Module 02

AR Guidance Module (Active Disaster)

When floodwaters rise, the mobile app utilizes AR camera overlays to guide victims safely through zero-visibility environments.

Live AR Path Overlay: Green 3D arrows projected on screen to show safe ground.
Danger Zone Warnings: Red highlighted areas indicating deep water or current risk.
Shelter Waypoints: Real-time distance and direction to nearest government relief camp.
Offline Mesh Capability: Functions even when cellular towers fail using pre-downloaded maps.

Target Real-World Deployment

Bihar Flood Belt Real-World Use Case

Villages and districts in flood-prone states like Bihar (Kosi, Gandak & Ganga floodplains) face annual severe inundation. JANRASHKAK is designed for low-resource environments:

  • Early Mobile SMS Warnings: Ensures alerts reach feature phones even without smartphones.
  • School & Community VR Hubs: Cost-effective Google Cardboard headsets for local panchayats.
  • AR Wayfinding: Prevents victims from wandering into hidden underwater ditches or live wires.
